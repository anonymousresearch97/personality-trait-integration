RESOLUTION OF THE REMAINING CHECKS

Categorical-alpha verification: completed computationally.
The categorical PC-stable analyses were independently recomputed at alpha
.01, .05 and .10 for the fixed full samples. All 27 fits (including the
historical primary childhood group for audit only) matched production code.
SAPA counts for <=18 / 19-39 / 40-59 / >=60 were 4/2/3/2 at .01 and 2/0/0/0
at both .05 and .10. The unusual ordering is reproducible: alpha changes
earlier edge removals and therefore the conditioning sets available later.
The fresh figures, exact edge lists and complete test traces are supplied.
These are single full-sample fits, not bootstraps. The original SAPA alpha
image was unavailable, so the supplied figure is a verified reconstruction,
not a claim that the unavailable original image was inspected. Sparse-table
asymptotic G-squared limitations remain acknowledged.
