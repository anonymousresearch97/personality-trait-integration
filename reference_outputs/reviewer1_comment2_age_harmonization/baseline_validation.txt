Baseline verification before interpreting the regrouped results

The existing cached analytic domain-score files were loaded with pandas and
discretized using sapa_hexaco_pc_bic.discretize_equal_width. Networks were
re-estimated using sapa_hexaco_pc_bic.pc_stable_edges (alpha=.05) and
hexaco_bic_exhaustive.exact_bic_dag, followed by skeleton extraction.

groups, sample sizes and the full PC and BIC skeleton edge sets matched the
saved results exactly. Every BIC score difference was 0.0.
