Measurement resolution plan — 10 November 2026
Fixed before examining any new factor-model fit statistics.

Scope: primary adolescence/adulthood only, age13–19/20–39/40–64/65–100.
Retain the established sample,96 keyed domain items and six16-item means.
Exclude childhood from the principal argument; retain its descriptive audit.
No network re-estimation, resampling expansion or score selection is planned.

Fit one predetermined measurement model per HEXACO domain:16 items loading
on their four named facets, with those four facets loading on their domain.
No cross-loadings or correlated item/facet residuals are added. A marker
loading fixes each factor's scale. First fit the model separately per age
group and jointly with age-specific loadings (configural). Then constrain
all non-marker item-to-facet AND facet-to-domain loadings equal across the
four groups (metric). Observed intercepts, item residual variances, facet
disturbance variances and domain variances remain age-specific. Latent means
are fixed at zero; no latent mean comparisons or scalar-invariance claims.

Use lavaan with robust maximum likelihood (MLR) on the five-category keyed
responses treated as numeric. This parallels the continuous-score analysis
and adjusts the test statistics for nonnormality; it is not an ordinal
threshold-invariance test. Report robust CFI/TLI/RMSEA and ordinary SRMR,
normal-theory values for audit, convergence, variance admissibility and
group-specific configural fit. Preserve the source item/facet map.

Interpretation is bounded: domain-wise item/facet measurement, not the full
six-domain cross-loading/discriminant structure and not equality of score
precision. Reliability alone is not invariant measurement. No item deletion,
modification-index search, ad-hoc correlated residuals, alternative parcels,
or partial-invariance search will be used to obtain a favorable result.

Descriptive fit screens, not universal hypothesis-test thresholds:
configural robust CFI>=.90, robust RMSEA<=.08 and SRMR<=.08 in EVERY age
group, plus convergence and admissibility. Report stricter.95/.06 context
through the numerical table rather than treating.90 as proof. For metric
models, report changes; deterioration beyond.010 in CFI,.015 in RMSEA or
.030 in SRMR flags sensitivity. An inadequate configural fit or improper
solution prevents a positive invariance claim even if changes are small.
Large-N chi-square significance will be reported, not used alone to decide.

SAPA: verify the raw-item reconstruction, actual age bands, per-person item
and facet coverage, and pairwise item overlap. Do not impute unavailable
responses or label pairwise-covariance alpha as reliability of each variable
short score. Use these findings to state the limits of external comparison.
No unsupported claim that this sparse design establishes invariance.

Deliver a concise measurement/results table and proposed manuscript/reviewer
wording. Existing manuscript source has not been located; do not claim the
submitted paper or figures have been edited when only replacement artifacts
and text are prepared.
